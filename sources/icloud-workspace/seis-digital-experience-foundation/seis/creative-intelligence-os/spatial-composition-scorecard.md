# Spatial Composition Scorecard

Use this scorecard for portfolio screens.

## Scores

- 5: Calm, spacious, clear, emotionally coherent
- 4: Strong rhythm with small refinements needed
- 3: Functional but visually ordinary
- 2: Cluttered, compressed, or inconsistent
- 1: Noisy, unstable, or hard to read

## Review Areas

- First viewport signal
- Heading rhythm
- Image/text balance
- Section breathing room
- Mobile spacing
- Interaction calmness
- Motion restraint
- Accessibility confidence

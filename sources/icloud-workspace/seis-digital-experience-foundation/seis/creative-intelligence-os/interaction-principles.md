# Interaction Principles

Interactions should feel quiet, deliberate, and useful.

## Principles

- Hover states should confirm affordance, not perform.
- Transitions should connect context, not steal attention.
- Navigation should feel continuous and calm.
- Focus states should be visible and elegant.
- Touch targets should remain comfortable on mobile.

## Portfolio-Specific Guidance

The work should remain the main event. Interaction design should frame the work with confidence and then get out of the way.

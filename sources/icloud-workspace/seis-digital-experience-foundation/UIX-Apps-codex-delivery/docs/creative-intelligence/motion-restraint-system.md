# Motion Restraint System

Motion should feel like calm breathing.

## Approved Motion

- opacity reveal
- small translate reveal
- subtle blur reveal
- slow atmospheric transitions
- restrained hover feedback

## Avoid

- bounce-heavy animation
- constant looping attention effects
- large motion shifts on text-heavy sections
- animations that delay reading
- effects that ignore reduced-motion preferences

## Performance Rule

Prefer transform and opacity. Motion must never damage readability or mobile responsiveness.

# Design Civilization Doctrine

SEIS uses cinematic minimalism, editorial rhythm, and humane pacing as production constraints.

- Preserve whitespace before adding features.
- Prefer compositional clarity over visual density.
- Treat motion as guidance, not entertainment.
- Keep every section readable without animation.

The design system should feel premium because it is calm, structured, and trustworthy.

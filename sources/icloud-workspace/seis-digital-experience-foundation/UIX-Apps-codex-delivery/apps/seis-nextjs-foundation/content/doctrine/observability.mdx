# Humane Observability Doctrine

SEIS observes health, accessibility, rendering consistency, and runtime queues with clear signals.

- Track fewer metrics with better explanations.
- Prefer watch states over panic states.
- Keep alert paths actionable.
- Separate user experience signals from internal operational noise.

The goal is operational trust, not telemetry volume.

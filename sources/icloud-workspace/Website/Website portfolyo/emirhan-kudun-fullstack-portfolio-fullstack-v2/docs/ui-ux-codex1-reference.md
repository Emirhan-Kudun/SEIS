## Observed UI Patterns

- Mobile-first hierarchy with one strong primary action per view.
- Large touch targets and clear icon/button separation.
- Dense social-screen inspiration, translated into calm rhythm rather than
  visual noise.
- Product cards use strong framing, predictable spacing, and compact metadata.
- Dark screens work best when contrast is controlled and text blocks stay short.
- Premium motion is subtle: state changes, hover elevation, and focus clarity
  matter more than decorative animation.

## Portfolio Translation

- Keep the first viewport calm and readable, with CTAs easy to reach on mobile.
- Use minimum comfortable hit areas for buttons, filters, and small controls.
- Keep cards visually stable with consistent padding, borders, and focus states.
- Avoid adding screenshot galleries or third-party inspiration content.
- Preserve the dark editorial identity while improving contrast and spacing.
- Treat motion as progressive enhancement; reduced-motion remains fully usable.

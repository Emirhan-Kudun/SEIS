# Code of Conduct

SEIS is built on humane technology principles (V14 §45). Everyone participating
in SEIS spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any open module — is
expected to uphold this code.

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background or identity.

**Encouraged:** clear and kind communication, good-faith review, accepting
constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the ecosystem, respecting
others' time and attention.

**Unacceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language,
publishing others' private information, dark patterns, deliberately disruptive
behavior, and any abuse of automation or access.

## Scope

This applies within all SEIS project spaces and when an individual is
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer:
**emirhankudun@gmail.com**. Reports are reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may
warn, remove contributions, or ban participants whose behavior violates this
code, and will respect the privacy and safety of reporters.
